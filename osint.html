Learn & interpret

What OSINT can tell you

Open-source intelligence uses information available from public sources. It can add context, but it rarely proves who controls a site or whether a message is genuine.

  • Check the source and time. Public databases can lag or disagree.
  • “No detection” is not a guarantee. New, targeted, or unreported threats may not appear.
  • Confirm through a separate trusted route. Use a known official app or contact channel.
  • Respect privacy and permission. Do not investigate private accounts or expose other people’s data.
