M-Pesa & mobile money

Fake reversals and payment claims

Do not send money back because of a caller’s instructions or a screenshot. Check your own account balance and transaction history, then contact the provider through its official app or website if something is unclear.

Phishing & suspicious links

Unexpected links and account alerts

A message may copy a real organisation’s name while sending you to a lookalike page. Open the official app or type a known website address yourself instead of following an unexpected link.

Jobs & advance fees

Pay-first job offers

Be cautious when a job offer demands a registration, training, processing, or placement fee before work begins. Verify the organisation and vacancy using contact details you find independently.

Online shops & marketplaces

Pressure to pay outside a platform

Compare the seller’s details, return terms, and independent reviews. Avoid rushed transfers to personal accounts when the marketplace offers safer payment protections.

Investment & impersonation

Guaranteed returns and trusted names

Promises of certain, unusually high returns and pressure to recruit friends deserve careful checking. Confirm a firm or person through the relevant regulator’s official directory before sending funds.

Passwords & account safety

Protect sign-in and recovery details

Use a unique password for each important account and turn on multi-factor authentication where available. Never share a PIN, password, or one-time code with a caller or message sender.
